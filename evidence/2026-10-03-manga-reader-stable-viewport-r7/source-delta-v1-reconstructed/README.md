These files were reconstructed after R7 evidence-directory drift. They are not presented as the original metadata directory. The deterministic source archive, its per-file inventory, the complete effective source inventory and the final version 1 freeze declaration match the exact hashes recorded before the drift. `RECONSTRUCTION_RECORD.json` distinguishes reconstructed records from unavailable originals.

The original version 1 `delta-manifest.json` and `comparison.json` contents remain unavailable. Their originally recorded hashes are retained in the first-attempt receipt and reconstruction report; their bytes are not recreated or fabricated. This limitation does not change the frozen production or test inputs, final version 2 archive, signed HAP or canonical input verification.
