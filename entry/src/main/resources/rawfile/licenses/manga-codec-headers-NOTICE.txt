Reader manga codec header inspection

zune-jpeg 0.5.15 and zune-core 0.5.1
Source: https://github.com/etemesi254/zune-image
Archives: https://static.crates.io/crates/zune-jpeg/zune-jpeg-0.5.15.crate
          https://static.crates.io/crates/zune-core/zune-core-0.5.1.crate
Reader uses the MIT license option for both packages.

Reader uses upstream header parsing only, with a read-only getter added to
zune-jpeg for allocation classification and an exact local zune-core dependency.
Reader-Core-Native/third_party retains source, archive hashes and patch notes.
Platform ImageKit continues to perform pixel decoding.
